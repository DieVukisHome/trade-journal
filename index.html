Welcome to Trade Journal

A mobile-first, local-only journaling workflow built around trade screenshots.

1. Screenshot in, journal almost done.

Import an open or closed trade screenshot and let OCR prefill the draft.

2. Your data stays local.

Trade data and screenshots remain on this device in this build. There is no upload server behind the workflow.

3. Open and Closed show different details.

Open screenshots focus on setup. Closed screenshots add result and final PnL information.

4. Built for a real mobile routine.

Save the trade now, review it later, and keep the journal habit alive.
